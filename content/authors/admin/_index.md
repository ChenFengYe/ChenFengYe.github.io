I am a Senior Research Scientist and Tech Leader at [ByteDance](https://www.bytedance.com/en/) in Bay Area, USA, working on large-scale video generation, including [Seedance](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/seedance2_0). Previously, I was a Research Scientist T10 at [Tencent](https://www.tencent.com/) working with [Dr. Gang Yu](https://www.skicyyu.org/). I received my Ph.D. from the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://english.ucas.ac.cn/) under the supervision of [Prof. Jingyi Yu](http://www.yu-jingyi.com/cv/), and was earlier supervised by [Prof. Youyi Zheng](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Nc5xuioAAAAJ&hl=en) at ShanghaiTech University.

My research interests focus on large-scale video generation, audio-visual foundation models, and unified multi-modal models. I have a great passion on new things and new ideas, my goal is to create Generative AI which is about humans, used for humans, and benefits humans.
